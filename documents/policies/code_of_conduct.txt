Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism 
at all times. Harassment, discrimination, or any form of workplace misconduct will not be tolerated 
and may result in disciplinary action, including termination. Employees must avoid conflicts of 
interest and disclose any outside business activities that may compete with company interests. 
Confidential company information must not be shared with external parties without authorization.
