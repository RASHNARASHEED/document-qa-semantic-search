Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business-related expenses, including travel, client 
meals, and work-related software subscriptions. All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the 
expense, along with valid receipts, through the finance portal. Claims exceeding 5000 rupees require 
prior written approval from a department head. Reimbursements are typically processed within 10 
working days of approval.
